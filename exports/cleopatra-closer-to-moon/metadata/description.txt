Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the building of the Great Pyramid. 🌙🏜️

We lump them together — pharaohs, pyramids, Cleopatra, all "ancient Egypt." But the Great Pyramid was built around 2,560 BC. Cleopatra ruled around 30 BC. We landed on the Moon in 1969.

That means the pyramids were already ~2,500 years old when Cleopatra was born — and she lived about 500 years closer to our Moon landing than to the pyramids being built.

The pyramids were as old to Cleopatra as she is to us. Time is deeper than it feels.
