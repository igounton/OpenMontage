You feel like you're in control — but your brain may decide before "you" do.

In 1983, Benjamin Libet found the brain's "readiness potential" fires about a third of a second before you're aware of choosing. Decades later, fMRI pushed it to 7 seconds. The feeling of deciding might be a story your brain tells after the fact.

You don't pull the trigger. You just find out that you did.
