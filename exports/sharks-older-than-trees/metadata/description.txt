Sharks are older than trees. 🦈🌲

Not older than some trees — older than the very first tree that ever grew on Earth. Sharks appeared around 450 million years ago. The first true trees only showed up about 350 million years ago. Sharks beat them by 100 million years.

They're older than Saturn's rings. Older than the dinosaurs. And they've survived all five mass extinctions that wiped out almost everything else.

So next time you stand in an ancient forest — remember, something in the sea is far, far older.
